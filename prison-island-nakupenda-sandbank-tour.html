Prison Island and Nakupenda Sandbank

Overview

Embark on a full-day adventure that combines history, wildlife, and pure tropical bliss. Begin your journey at Prison Island, a site rich with stories from Zanzibar’s past and home to the famous giant Aldabra tortoises. Guests will enjoy a guided exploration of the island’s historical ruins and natural beauty.

Continue your experience with a scenic boat ride to Nakupenda Sandbank, a breathtaking stretch of white sand surrounded by turquoise waters. Here, guests can relax, swim, and snorkel in the crystal-clear ocean while soaking up the sun. The sandbank offers a perfect escape into paradise, ideal for both adventure seekers and those craving tranquility.

This tour blends cultural discovery with natural wonder, ensuring guests leave with unforgettable memories. From the fascinating history of Prison Island to the idyllic beauty of Nakupenda Sandbank, it’s a journey that captures the essence of Zanzibar’s charm and allure.

Highlights
  • Guided exploration of Prison Island’s historic ruins
  • Encounter with giant Aldabra tortoises
  • Scenic boat ride across turquoise waters
  • Relaxation and swimming at Nakupenda Sandbank
  • Snorkeling opportunities in crystal-clear ocean
Itinerary

Your adventure begins with a boat transfer to Prison Island, where guests are welcomed by stunning coastal views. Upon arrival, you’ll explore the island’s historical buildings and learn about its fascinating past. The highlight is meeting the giant Aldabra tortoises, a unique wildlife encounter that delights visitors of all ages.

Next, the journey continues with a boat ride to Nakupenda Sandbank, a pristine paradise surrounded by the Indian Ocean. Guests can relax on the soft white sand, swim in the warm waters, or snorkel among vibrant marine life. The sandbank offers a perfect blend of relaxation and adventure, making it an unforgettable stop.

Finally, the tour concludes with time to unwind and soak in the beauty of Nakupenda before returning to Zanzibar. Guests leave with lasting impressions of both the island’s cultural heritage and its natural wonders, making this full-day experience truly special.
