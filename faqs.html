Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions?

Zanzibar is a tropical island destination with warm weather, beach adventures, and a rich Swahili culture. Travelers should carry a valid passport, have their visa or entry requirements sorted in advance, and keep some cash for local markets, small shops, and tips. It is also wise to wear light clothing, use sun protection, and stay hydrated during tours.

Yes, we strongly recommend booking in advance, especially during peak travel seasons, public holidays, and weekends. Advance booking helps secure your preferred tour date, transport arrangement, and any special meals or group requirements. It also gives us time to prepare your itinerary and support you with the right experience.

We recommend bringing comfortable lightweight clothes, closed walking shoes, swimwear, a hat, sunglasses, sunscreen, a reusable water bottle, and a light rain jacket. If you are joining snorkeling, boat, or beach activities, a dry bag, extra towel, and waterproof phone pouch are helpful. For safari trips, binoculars and a camera are excellent extras.

Zanzibar is generally safe for tourists, and most visitors enjoy a smooth and welcoming stay. We still advise that you respect local traditions, dress modestly in villages and towns, avoid isolated areas at night, and keep valuables secure. Our guides will give you practical safety tips throughout the tour and help you enjoy the island responsibly.

The best time to visit Zanzibar depends on your interests. June to October is popular for warm, sunny conditions and great safari connections, while December to February is ideal for beach holidays and clear, tropical weather. If you are planning snorkeling and marine experiences, calm ocean conditions are often better during the dry season.

Weather can affect boat rides, snorkeling, and some outdoor activities. In such cases, Ukindu Tour and Safari will advise the safest option, which may include a schedule change, alternate route, or rearranged activity. We always prioritize guest comfort and safety, and our team will communicate any updates clearly before or during the trip.

Cards are accepted in many hotels and larger businesses, but cash is still very useful for local markets, smaller restaurants, tolls, transportation, and tips. We recommend carrying a small amount of Tanzanian shillings and some US dollars in smaller denominations for convenience.

Ukindu Tour and Safari is committed to providing safe, well-organized, and memorable Zanzibar tours. Our team helps you plan your adventure, guides you through cultural and nature experiences, and ensures your tour is comfortable and locally informed. We focus on genuine hospitality, good communication, and a smooth guest experience from booking to return.
