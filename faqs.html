Before departure

Travel planning

Practical preparation for your Tanzania and Zanzibar trip.

We recommend arranging suitable travel insurance for your trip. Check the policy wording, coverage limits and activities covered with your insurer before travel.

Health recommendations depend on your itinerary and personal circumstances. Speak with a qualified travel-health professional and check current guidance from an official source such as the CDC Tanzania traveler page.

The Tanzanian shilling is the local currency. Carrying some local cash can be useful, but payment options vary by business and location. Check with your bank about card access and fees before travel.

Card acceptance varies between hotels, restaurants and smaller businesses. Ask your accommodation about its payment options and carry an alternative payment method for places that do not accept cards.

Coverage and internet access vary by location, accommodation and provider, especially on safari routes. Check roaming costs with your mobile provider and ask your accommodation about connectivity.

Start times depend on the itinerary, park and planned activity. Your day-by-day schedule will show the planned timing; ask us to clarify it if you have an early flight or other connection.

If you have arranged a transfer with us, share your flight details and accommodation with the team in advance and confirm the pickup plan. For independent travel, follow airport signage and use transport you have arranged.

Swahili is widely spoken, and English is commonly used in tourism. Your itinerary details can help confirm the language support available for your specific tour.

Yes. Requirements depend on your nationality and can change. Check your passport validity, visa and entry guidance with Tanzania Immigration and your airline before departure.

Practical reassurance

Safety & comfort

Plan thoughtfully, follow local guidance and confirm the arrangements that matter to you.

Stay aware of your surroundings, keep valuables secure and follow local guidance. Check your government’s current travel advice before departure and ask your accommodation or local host about area-specific guidance.

Follow your guide’s instructions promptly, especially around wildlife, vehicles and park rules. If you are unsure what to do, ask your guide to explain before moving.

Keep a respectful distance, stay in the vehicle when instructed and never approach, feed or call wild animals. Your guide can advise what is appropriate at each sighting.

Emergency procedures depend on where you are and the activity. Before travel, confirm the trip contact process, emergency arrangements and your insurance provider’s assistance details with the relevant providers.

As in any destination, stay aware of your surroundings and follow local guidance. Zanzibar has a strong cultural and religious heritage; modest dress away from beaches and respect for local customs are appreciated.

Family travel depends on the child’s age, your chosen activities and each accommodation’s rules. Share the children’s ages with us so we can discuss a suitable itinerary.
