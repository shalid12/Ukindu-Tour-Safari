Tour Overview

Embark on one of Zanzibar’s most iconic full-day excursions, the Safari Blue Trip, where adventure meets relaxation in the turquoise waters of the Indian Ocean. Guests will enjoy sailing on traditional dhows, snorkeling in vibrant coral reefs, and exploring pristine sandbanks surrounded by breathtaking scenery.

This experience offers a perfect blend of marine exploration and leisure. Guests can swim in natural lagoons, discover colorful tropical fish, and unwind on untouched beaches. The tour is designed to showcase the beauty of Zanzibar’s coastline while providing unforgettable moments of discovery and fun.

Conclude the day with a delicious seafood feast served on a remote island, accompanied by refreshing tropical fruits. The Safari Blue Trip is more than just a tour—it’s a celebration of Zanzibar’s natural wonders, culture, and hospitality, leaving guests with lasting memories of paradise.

Tour Highlights

  • Traditional dhow sailing across turquoise waters
  • Snorkeling in coral reefs with tropical marine life
  • Swimming in natural lagoons and sandbanks
  • Fresh seafood lunch with tropical fruits
  • Relaxation on pristine beaches and islands

Step-by-Step Itinerary

Stop 01 · Departure

Hotel Pickup & Departure

Your adventure begins with a dhow cruise across the sparkling waters of Zanzibar’s southern coast. Guests are welcomed by stunning ocean views as the journey unfolds toward sandbanks and lagoons.

Stop 02 · Highlight

Guided Tour & Main Experience

Next, the tour offers snorkeling opportunities in coral reefs teeming with marine life. Guests can swim alongside colorful fish and explore the underwater beauty of the Indian Ocean. The experience is guided, ensuring both safety and enjoyment for all participants.

Stop 03 · Discovery

Exploration, Leisure & Return

Finally, the day concludes with a seafood banquet served on a remote island, followed by time to relax on the beach or swim in natural lagoons. Guests return with unforgettable memories of Zanzibar’s marine paradise, having experienced the perfect balance of adventure and relaxation.

Know Before You Go

What to Bring

Pack swimwear, a beach towel, reef-safe sunscreen, sunglasses, a sun hat, and a waterproof pouch for your phone or camera.

Best Departure Times

Morning departures offer the calmest Indian Ocean waters, clearest snorkeling visibility, and most comfortable temperatures.

Safety & Comfort

Life jackets and safety briefings are provided on all boats. Non-swimmers and families with children are warmly welcomed and assisted.

Flexible Hotel Pickup

We arrange direct door-to-door pickup from Stone Town, Nungwi, Kendwa, Paje, Jambiani, Kiwengwa, and all Zanzibar beach resorts.
