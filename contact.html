Quick Answers

Questions Before You Reach Out?

A few helpful answers—or explore our full Travel FAQs page.

How quickly do you reply to enquiries?

On WhatsApp, our Nungwi team typically replies in under 15 minutes during the day. Email enquiries are answered within a few hours.

Can I combine Zanzibar tours with a Tanzania safari?

Yes! Combining mainland wildlife parks like Serengeti, Ngorongoro, or Mikumi with a Zanzibar beach stay is our most popular request.

Are your tours and transfers private?

Yes—all airport transfers, island tours, and safaris can be arranged privately around your hotel location, schedule, and preferred pace.

What details should I include in my message?

Share your approximate travel dates, number of travelers, hotel area (if booked), and the experiences you’re interested in. It’s completely fine if you’re still deciding!
