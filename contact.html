Before you get in touch

Questions before you start?

View all FAQs
How far in advance should I book?

It depends on your travel dates, route and accommodation preferences. Contact us with the dates you have in mind and we can discuss availability.

Can you create a custom safari?

Yes. Share your interests, dates and preferred pace and our team can discuss a tailor-made safari itinerary with you.

Can I combine a safari with Zanzibar?

Yes. We plan journeys that combine mainland safari experiences with time in Zanzibar. Tell us how many days you have available.

Do you offer private safaris?

Private safari journeys are among the trips our team plans. Let us know your group size and the experience you are looking for.

How much does a Tanzania safari cost?

The total depends on your route, travel dates, accommodation and group size. Send us your preferences and we can discuss options.

What information do you need from me?

Start with your approximate dates, number of travelers and the experiences that interest you. It is fine if some details are still undecided.
