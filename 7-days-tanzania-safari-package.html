Tour Overview

Embark on a 7 days safari adventure through Tanzania’s most iconic destinations: Tarangire National Park, Serengeti National Park, Ngorongoro Crater, and Lake Manyara National Park. This journey blends breathtaking scenery, abundant wildlife, and cultural immersion, offering guests a truly authentic safari experience.

Tarangire is renowned for its vast elephant herds and ancient baobab trees, while the Serengeti captivates with endless plains and the legendary Great Migration. The Ngorongoro Crater, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, provides one of the most dramatic wildlife viewing experiences in Africa. Lake Manyara adds diversity with its lush forests, flamingos, and tree‑climbing lions.

From arrival to departure, guests are guided by professional safari experts who ensure comfort, safety, and storytelling throughout the journey. Whether it’s spotting lions on the crater floor, witnessing wildebeest herds in the Serengeti, or elephants in Tarangire, this safari promises unforgettable memories.

Tour Highlights

  • Explore Tarangire National Park, famous for elephants and baobab trees
  • Discover Serengeti National Park with its endless plains and Great Migration
  • Experience the UNESCO listed Ngorongoro Crater, teeming with wildlife
  • Visit Lake Manyara National Park, home to flamingos and tree climbing lions
  • Spot lions, zebras, wildebeest, giraffes, hippos, and more in natural habitats
  • Guided safari drives with professional experts

Step-by-Step Itinerary

Day 01 · Safari

Arrival in Tanzania

Guests are warmly welcomed upon arrival and transferred to their accommodation. The evening is spent relaxing and preparing for the safari adventure ahead.

Day 02 · Safari

Tarangire National Park

The journey begins with a drive to Tarangire, where guests enjoy game drives across vast plains dotted with baobab trees. Wildlife encounters include elephants, giraffes, zebras, and predators roaming freely.

Stop 03 · Tour

Day 3 & 4: Serengeti National Park

Guests spend two days exploring the Serengeti, renowned for its sweeping landscapes and incredible wildlife. Highlights include witnessing the Great Migration (seasonal), spotting predators, and enjoying the vast diversity of animals across the plains.

Day 05 · Safari

Ngorongoro Crater

Guests descend into the Ngorongoro Crater, a natural wonder teeming with wildlife. The crater floor offers exceptional opportunities to spot lions, wildebeest, hippos, and rare black rhinos, all within a dramatic volcanic landscape.

Day 06 · Safari

Lake Manyara National Park

Guests explore Lake Manyara, a park known for its diverse habitats and wildlife. Highlights include tree‑climbing lions, large flocks of flamingos, hippos, and stunning views of the Rift Valley escarpment.

Day 07 · Safari

Departure

The safari concludes with a return journey, allowing guests to reflect on their adventure before departing. Memories of Tanzania’s wildlife and landscapes remain long after the trip ends.

Know Before You Go

Pack Smart & Light

Bring soft-sided luggage (15kg limit on bush flights), neutral-colored breathable layers, a light fleece for early mornings, and comfortable closed shoes.

Wildlife & Photography

Pack binoculars, a zoom lens, extra camera batteries, sunscreen, sunglasses, and insect repellent for full days in the parks.

Seamless Zanzibar Flights

We coordinate your Zanzibar hotel transfers, domestic bush flights, park permits, and 4x4 safari vehicle so every connection runs smoothly.

Documents & Dietary Needs

Carry your passport for domestic flights and park gates. Let us know any dietary preferences or special occasions in advance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can this 7 Days 6 Nights Great Tanzania Safari start directly from my Zanzibar hotel?

Yes! We handle everything seamlessly from your Zanzibar resort—including early-morning airport transfer, domestic flights to the mainland airstrip, private 4x4 safari vehicle, and return transfer.

Are park entry fees and game drives included?

Yes, all national park entry fees, conservation fees, private 4x4 pop-up roof Land Cruiser, and professional safari guide are included in your tailored quote.

Can we customize the accommodation level or dates?

Absolutely. Every Ukindu safari is 100% private and flexible—choose midrange lodges, luxury tented camps, or honeymoon upgrades on your preferred travel dates.

What is the luggage limit for bush flights from Zanzibar?

Domestic safari flights generally allow 15 kg (33 lbs) per person in a soft-sided duffel bag. Extra luggage can be safely stored at your Zanzibar hotel or our office.
