Guests are welcomed into a tranquil environment where conservation meets education, offering a rare opportunity to interact with these gentle creatures.

As you explore the aquarium, you’ll learn about the efforts to protect and rehabilitate sea turtles, guided by local experts passionate about marine conservation. The experience is both informative and inspiring, allowing guests to connect with nature while supporting sustainable tourism.

This tour is perfect for families, nature lovers, and anyone seeking an authentic encounter with Zanzibar’s wildlife. With its blend of conservation, culture, and relaxation, the Baraka Aquarium Tour leaves guests with lasting memories of the island’s commitment to preserving its natural heritage.

Highlights
  • Visit Baraka Natural Aquarium, home to rescued sea turtles
  • Learn about marine conservation and rehabilitation efforts
  • Feed and interact with turtles in a safe environment
  • Enjoy a guided educational experience with local experts
  • Relax in a serene coastal setting surrounded by nature
Itinerary

The tour begins with a warm welcome at Baraka Aquarium, where guests are introduced to the site’s mission and conservation efforts. The guide sets the stage for an engaging and educational experience.

Guests then explore the aquarium, observing turtles in their natural lagoon habitat. With opportunities to feed and interact, the experience is both hands‑on and enriching, offering insights into the life cycle and challenges faced by these marine creatures.

The tour concludes with time to relax by the lagoon, reflect on the conservation message, and capture memorable photos. Guests leave with a deeper appreciation of Zanzibar’s marine life and the importance of protecting it for future generations.
