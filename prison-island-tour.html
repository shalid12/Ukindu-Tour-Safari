Prison Island / Changuu Island Tour

Overview

Embark on a captivating half-day adventure to Prison Island, also known as Changuu Island, one of Zanzibar’s most iconic attractions. This tour blends history, nature, and relaxation, offering travelers a chance to explore the island’s fascinating past while enjoying its serene beauty. Perfect for those seeking a short yet enriching escape, it’s an ideal addition to your Zanzibar itinerary.

Discover the island’s intriguing history as you walk through remnants of the old prison buildings, originally built in the 19th century. Learn about its transformation from a detention site to a quarantine station, and how it eventually became a sanctuary for giant Aldabra tortoises. Every corner of the island tells a story, making it a must-visit for history enthusiasts and curious explorers alike.

Beyond its historical significance, Prison Island offers breathtaking natural scenery. From crystal-clear turquoise waters to lush vegetation, the island is a paradise for nature lovers. Guests can enjoy snorkeling in vibrant coral reefs, relax on pristine beaches, and marvel at the giant tortoises that roam freely. This tour promises a blend of culture, wildlife, and leisure in one unforgettable experience.

Highlights
  • Visit the historic prison ruins and learn about Zanzibar’s colonial past.
  • Encounter giant Aldabra tortoises, some over 100 years old.
  • Snorkel in crystal-clear waters with colorful coral reefs.
  • Relax on white sandy beaches surrounded by turquoise seas.
  • Enjoy panoramic views of Stone Town from the island.
Itinerary

Your journey begins with a scenic boat ride from Stone Town, gliding across the sparkling waters of the Indian Ocean. As you approach Changuu Island, the sight of lush greenery and historic ruins sets the tone for an exciting exploration ahead.

Upon arrival, guests are welcomed by the island’s unique charm. The first stop is the historic prison building, where guides share captivating stories about its past. From there, you’ll stroll through the tortoise sanctuary, where giant Aldabra tortoises roam freely, offering a rare chance to interact with these gentle giants.

The adventure continues with leisure and discovery. Guests can snorkel in the surrounding coral reefs, teeming with marine life, or simply relax on the beach and soak in the sun. The tour concludes with time to unwind, take photos, and enjoy the tranquil atmosphere before returning to Stone Town by boat, leaving you with lasting memories of Zanzibar’s natural and cultural treasures.
