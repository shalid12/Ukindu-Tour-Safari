6 Days Tanzania Safari

Overview

Embark on a 6 days safari adventure through three of Tanzania’s most celebrated destinations: Tarangire National Park, Serengeti National Park, and the Ngorongoro Crater. This journey combines breathtaking scenery, abundant wildlife, and cultural immersion, offering guests a truly authentic safari experience.

Tarangire National Park is renowned for its vast elephant herds and iconic baobab trees, while the Serengeti captivates with endless plains and the legendary Great Migration. The Ngorongoro Crater, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, provides one of the most dramatic wildlife viewing experiences in Africa.

From arrival to departure, guests are guided by professional safari experts who ensure comfort, safety, and storytelling throughout the journey. Whether it’s spotting lions on the crater floor, witnessing wildebeest herds in the Serengeti, or elephants in Tarangire, this safari promises unforgettable memories.

Highlights
  • Explore Tarangire National Park, famous for elephants and baobab trees
  • Discover Serengeti National Park with its endless plains and Great Migration
  • Experience the UNESCO‑listed Ngorongoro Crater, teeming with wildlife
  • Spot lions, zebras, wildebeest, giraffes, hippos, and more in natural habitats
  • Guided safari drives with professional experts
  • Scenic landscapes and cultural immersion in Tanzania’s countryside
Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Tanzania
Guests are warmly welcomed upon arrival and transferred to their accommodation. The evening is spent relaxing and preparing for the safari adventure ahead.

Day 2: Tarangire National Park
The journey begins with a drive to Tarangire, where guests enjoy game drives across vast plains dotted with baobab trees. Wildlife encounters include elephants, giraffes, zebras, and predators roaming freely.

Day 3 & 4: Serengeti National Park
Guests spend two days exploring the Serengeti, renowned for its sweeping landscapes and incredible wildlife. Highlights include witnessing the Great Migration (seasonal), spotting predators, and enjoying the vast diversity of animals across the plains.

Day 5: Ngorongoro Crater
Guests descend into the Ngorongoro Crater, a natural wonder teeming with wildlife. The crater floor offers exceptional opportunities to spot lions, wildebeest, hippos, and rare black rhinos, all within a dramatic volcanic landscape.

Day 6: Departure
The safari concludes with a return journey, allowing guests to reflect on their adventure before departing. Memories of Tanzania’s wildlife and landscapes remain long after the trip ends.
