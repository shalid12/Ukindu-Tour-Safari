Spice Farm, Prison Island and Stone Town

Overview

Embark on a captivating full-day journey that immerses you in the rich history, culture, and natural beauty of Zanzibar. This tour combines three of the island’s most iconic experiences, offering guests a perfect blend of discovery, relaxation, and adventure.

Begin your day at a traditional spice farm, where the air is filled with the aroma of cloves, nutmeg, and cinnamon. Guests will enjoy an interactive exploration of Zanzibar’s spice heritage, learning how these treasures shaped the island’s global reputation. The farm visit is both educational and sensory, with opportunities to taste and smell fresh spices directly from the source.

Continue to Prison Island, a serene escape surrounded by turquoise waters. Famous for its giant Aldabra tortoises and historical significance, the island offers a mix of wildlife encounters and fascinating stories from the past. End the day in Stone Town, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, where winding alleys, bustling markets, and centuries-old architecture reveal the soul of Zanzibar.

Highlights
  • Guided exploration of a traditional spice farm with tastings
  • Visit to Prison Island and encounter with giant tortoises
  • Scenic boat ride across crystal-clear waters
  • Walking tour of Stone Town’s historic alleys and landmarks
  • Cultural immersion through local markets and Swahili heritage
Itinerary

Your adventure begins with a guided visit to a lush spice farm, where you’ll stroll through plantations and discover the secrets behind Zanzibar’s nickname, “The Spice Island.” Guests will learn about the cultivation and uses of spices while enjoying hands-on experiences that awaken the senses.

Next, a short boat ride takes you to Prison Island, a tranquil haven with a fascinating past. Here, you’ll meet the famous giant tortoises, explore remnants of historical buildings, and enjoy the island’s natural beauty. The combination of wildlife and history makes this stop both relaxing and enriching.

Finally, the tour concludes in Stone Town, the cultural heart of Zanzibar. Guests will wander through narrow streets lined with carved wooden doors, visit vibrant bazaars, and admire landmarks such as the Old Fort and House of Wonders. This final chapter of the journey offers a deep dive into Zanzibar’s heritage, leaving guests with lasting memories of its charm and character.
