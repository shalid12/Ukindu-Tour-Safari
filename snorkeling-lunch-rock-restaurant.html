Tour Overview

Embark on a half‑day journey that blends relaxation, culture, and culinary delight. This experience is designed to showcase one of Zanzibar’s most iconic dining spots while immersing you in the island’s coastal charm.

Begin with a scenic drive through lush landscapes and traditional villages, offering glimpses of everyday life in Zanzibar. The journey itself sets the tone for an authentic and memorable adventure.

Arrive at the world‑famous Rock Restaurant, perched dramatically on a coral outcrop in the Indian Ocean. Here, guests enjoy a unique dining atmosphere surrounded by turquoise waters, making it one of the most photographed and celebrated restaurants in the world.

Tour Highlights

  • Scenic drive through Zanzibar’s coastal villages
  • Exclusive dining at the Rock Restaurant, set on a coral rock in the ocean
  • Stunning panoramic views of the Indian Ocean
  • Opportunity to capture unforgettable photos of this iconic location
  • Relaxed half‑day experience, perfect for combining with other activities

Step-by-Step Itinerary

Stop 01 · Departure

Hotel Pickup & Departure

The tour begins with a comfortable pickup from your hotel, followed by a drive through Zanzibar’s picturesque countryside and coastal villages. Guests enjoy the sights and sounds of local life along the way, creating a sense of connection to the island.

Stop 02 · Highlight

Guided Tour & Main Experience

Upon arrival at the Rock Restaurant, you’ll be welcomed into one of the most unique dining venues in the world. Surrounded by the ocean, the restaurant offers a truly magical setting where you can savor freshly prepared seafood and local delicacies.

Stop 03 · Discovery

Exploration, Leisure & Return

After lunch, guests have time to relax, take photos, and soak in the breathtaking views before returning to their hotel. The experience concludes with a smooth transfer back, leaving you with lasting memories of Zanzibar’s coastal beauty and culinary excellence.

Know Before You Go

What to Bring

Pack swimwear, a beach towel, reef-safe sunscreen, sunglasses, a sun hat, and a waterproof pouch for your phone or camera.

Best Departure Times

Morning departures offer the calmest Indian Ocean waters, clearest snorkeling visibility, and most comfortable temperatures.

Safety & Comfort

Life jackets and safety briefings are provided on all boats. Non-swimmers and families with children are warmly welcomed and assisted.

Flexible Hotel Pickup

We arrange direct door-to-door pickup from Stone Town, Nungwi, Kendwa, Paje, Jambiani, Kiwengwa, and all Zanzibar beach resorts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer hotel pickup from Nungwi, Kendwa, Paje, or Stone Town?

Yes! We provide private air-conditioned transfers from every hotel and resort across Zanzibar. Share your hotel name when enquiring for exact pickup timing.

Is the Lunch at the Rock Restaurant private or shared with other groups?

We specialize in private, unhurried tours with your own dedicated local guide so you can enjoy each stop at your own pace. Shared options can also be arranged on request.

Is this experience suitable for families with children?

Yes, families and travelers of all ages are welcome. Our local guides tailor the pace, shade breaks, and assistance so everyone has a safe and memorable time.

What is your cancellation and booking policy?

No upfront payment is required to check availability, and we offer free cancellation or rescheduling up to 24 hours before your scheduled pickup.
