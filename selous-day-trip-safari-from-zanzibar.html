Selous Day Trip Safari From Zanzibar

Overview

Embark on a thrilling day trip from Zanzibar to the vast Selous Game Reserve, one of Africa’s largest protected wildlife areas. Guests will enjoy an authentic safari experience surrounded by diverse landscapes, ranging from open savannahs to riverine forests, offering incredible opportunities for wildlife encounters.

The reserve is home to elephants, lions, giraffes, hippos, crocodiles, and countless bird species. Guided by professional safari experts, guests will explore the natural habitats of these animals while learning about the unique ecosystem that makes Selous a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Conclude the day with unforgettable memories of Tanzania’s wilderness. The Selous Day Trip Safari offers the excitement of a full safari adventure in just one day, blending discovery, photography, and the raw beauty of Africa’s most iconic reserve.

Highlights
  • Full-day safari in Selous Game Reserve
  • Chance to spot elephants, lions, giraffes, hippos, and crocodiles
  • Guided game drives with professional safari guides
  • Scenic landscapes and diverse birdlife
  • Perfect for photography and wildlife enthusiasts
Itinerary

Your adventure begins with a transfer from Zanzibar to Selous Game Reserve, where guests are welcomed by the vast wilderness and breathtaking scenery. The journey itself sets the tone for an exciting day ahead.

Upon arrival, guests embark on guided game drives across the reserve. With the help of experienced guides, you’ll explore different habitats and encounter a variety of wildlife, from herds of elephants and giraffes to predators like lions and crocodiles along the riverbanks. The drives are designed to maximize sightings and provide an authentic safari experience.

Finally, the tour concludes with time to relax and reflect on the day’s encounters before returning to Zanzibar. Guests leave with unforgettable memories of Tanzania’s wildlife and the thrill of a true African safari adventure.
