Our Story

Rooted in Zanzibar. Made for meaningful journeys.

Ukindu Tour & Safari is based in Nungwi, Zanzibar. Our local team plans private Tanzania safaris, island excursions, transfers and tailor-made journeys that bring the mainland and the coast together.

We start by listening: your dates, the places you hope to see, and the pace that feels right. From there, we help connect the details into a clear, personal travel plan.
