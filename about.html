Our Story

Rooted in Zanzibar. Built for Unforgettable Journeys.

Ukindu Tour & Safari was founded in Nungwi, Zanzibar with a simple belief: the best way to experience East Africa is alongside passionate local hosts who know every reef, hidden sandbank, spice trail, and wildlife corridor personally.

Over the past 5+ years, our team has welcomed more than 450 travelers from across Europe, America, and around the world—arranging everything from half-day Stone Town walks and Mnemba snorkeling trips to private fly-in Serengeti safaris and 24/7 airport transfers.
