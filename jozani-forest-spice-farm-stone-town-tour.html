Jozani Forest, Spice Farm and Stone Town

Overview

Step into the natural wonders of Zanzibar with a guided exploration of Jozani Forest, home to the rare red colobus monkeys and a thriving mangrove ecosystem. This lush sanctuary offers guests a chance to connect with the island’s biodiversity while enjoying scenic trails and informative insights from expert guides.

Continue your adventure at a traditional spice farm, where the island’s agricultural heritage comes alive. Guests will stroll through aromatic plantations filled with cloves, nutmeg, cinnamon, and vanilla, learning how these spices shaped Zanzibar’s global reputation. The farm visit is interactive and sensory, with opportunities to taste, smell, and even purchase fresh spices directly from the source.

Conclude the day in Stone Town, a UNESCO World Heritage Site that embodies the cultural soul of Zanzibar. Wander through winding alleys, admire centuries-old architecture, and explore bustling markets filled with vibrant colors and sounds. This final chapter of the tour immerses guests in the island’s history and charm, leaving them with unforgettable memories.

Highlights
  • Guided exploration of Jozani Forest with red colobus monkeys
  • Educational and sensory spice farm tour with tastings
  • Insight into Zanzibar’s spice heritage and local traditions
  • Walking tour of Stone Town’s historic alleys and landmarks
  • Cultural immersion through markets, architecture, and Swahili heritage
Itinerary

Your adventure begins with a guided walk through Jozani Forest, where towering trees and mangrove boardwalks create a magical atmosphere. Guests will encounter the rare red colobus monkeys and learn about the forest’s vital role in Zanzibar’s ecosystem. The experience is both educational and inspiring, offering a glimpse into the island’s natural treasures.

Next, the journey continues to a spice farm, where guests are welcomed with warm hospitality. Strolling through plantations, you’ll discover the cultivation and uses of Zanzibar’s world-famous spices. The interactive tour allows guests to taste and smell fresh spices, making it a truly sensory experience that connects history with daily life.

Finally, the tour concludes in Stone Town, the cultural heart of Zanzibar. Guests will wander through narrow streets lined with carved wooden doors, visit vibrant bazaars, and admire landmarks such as the Old Fort and House of Wonders. This final stop offers a deep dive into Zanzibar’s heritage, blending history, culture, and everyday life into one unforgettable experience.
