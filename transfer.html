Good to know

Transfer frequently asked questions

How many people can travel in one vehicle?

The published airport and route tables are marked for up to 4 people. Contact us to discuss a larger group.

Are the prices per person or per vehicle?

The tables show the published transfer rates and note a limit of up to 4 people. Please confirm the pricing basis with our team when booking.

Can I book an airport pickup?

Yes. The airport transfer table lists prices from Zanzibar Airport to the destinations shown.

Can you pick me up from my hotel?

Hotel and destination connections are available on the published route table. Send us your pickup point and destination to confirm your route.

Can I book a return transfer?

Return rates are shown for the routes where a return price is listed. Contact us to confirm the details for your journey.

How do I pay for the transfer?

Please contact our team to confirm the payment arrangements for your transfer.
