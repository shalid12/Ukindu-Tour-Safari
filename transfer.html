Helpful Information

Transfer Frequently Asked Questions

How many people can travel in one vehicle?

The published service information mentions group transport but does not state a vehicle capacity. Share your passenger count so our team can confirm a suitable option.

Are the prices per person or per vehicle?

The price tables do not specify the pricing basis. Please confirm whether a listed fare is per person or per vehicle when arranging your transfer.

Can I book an airport pickup?

Yes. Airport pickups to the destinations listed in the airport transfer table are part of the published service. Share your flight details and destination when you contact us.

Can you pick me up from a hotel or ferry?

The published service information includes ferry transfers and transfers between hotels in Zanzibar. Send the exact pickup and drop-off locations so the team can confirm your route.

Can I book a return transfer?

Return fares are shown for routes where they are listed. For any return route that is not clear in the price tables, contact us to confirm availability and price.

What information do you need to arrange my transfer?

Please send your name, travel date and time, flight number if applicable, pickup location, destination, hotel or resort name, passenger count, and a WhatsApp or phone number.

How do I pay for the transfer?

Payment methods are not specified on this page. Contact our team to confirm the current payment instructions before making a payment.
