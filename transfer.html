Helpful Information

Transfer Frequently Asked Questions

How many people can travel in one vehicle?

We offer comfortable sedans, SUVs, and spacious group minivans depending on your party size and luggage. Share your passenger count when messaging us so we assign the ideal vehicle.

Are the prices per person or per vehicle?

Please confirm whether your listed route fare applies per vehicle or group size when arranging your transfer—we confirm every quote clearly in writing before pickup.

Can I book an airport pickup?

Yes. Airport pickups to all destinations listed in the airport transfer table are available 24/7. Share your flight details and hotel name when you contact us.

Can you pick me up from a hotel or ferry?

Yes, we arrange ferry terminal pickups and hotel-to-hotel transfers across all of Zanzibar. Send your exact pickup and drop-off locations so we can confirm your route.

Can I book a return transfer?

Return fares are shown for routes where listed. For any custom return transfer, contact us on WhatsApp to confirm availability and pricing.

How do I pay for the transfer?

We confirm flexible payment options (cash in USD/EUR/TZS or card/transfer) directly when confirming your booking.
