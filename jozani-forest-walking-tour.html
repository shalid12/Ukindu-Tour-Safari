Tour Overview

Discover the enchanting Jozani Forest, the largest and most biodiverse natural reserve in Zanzibar. This half-day walking tour invites guests to explore lush greenery, rare wildlife, and the island’s unique ecosystem. Perfect for nature lovers and adventure seekers, it offers an unforgettable journey into the heart of Zanzibar’s natural heritage.

The forest is home to the famous red colobus monkeys, an endangered species found only in Zanzibar. As you walk along shaded trails, you’ll encounter these playful primates in their natural habitat, along with a variety of birds, butterflies, and other fascinating creatures. The experience is both educational and inspiring, highlighting the importance of conservation.

Beyond wildlife, Jozani Forest showcases diverse landscapes, from dense tropical woodlands to mangrove swamps. Guests can enjoy the tranquility of the forest, breathe in fresh air, and connect with nature in a serene environment. This tour is a perfect escape from the bustle of town life, offering a deeper appreciation of Zanzibar’s ecological treasures.

Tour Highlights

  • Encounter the rare Zanzibar red colobus monkeys.
  • Explore lush tropical forest trails.
  • Discover mangrove swamps and their unique ecosystem.
  • Spot diverse bird species and butterflies.
  • Learn about conservation efforts in Zanzibar.

Step-by-Step Itinerary

Stop 01 · Departure

Hotel Pickup & Departure

The adventure begins with a guided walk through the shaded trails of Jozani Forest, where guests are introduced to the rich biodiversity of the reserve. The peaceful atmosphere sets the tone for an immersive nature experience.

Stop 02 · Highlight

Guided Tour & Main Experience

As the walk continues, guests encounter the famous red colobus monkeys, observing their playful behavior and learning about their conservation. The guide also points out other wildlife, including birds, butterflies, and small forest creatures, making the journey both educational and exciting.

Stop 03 · Discovery

Exploration, Leisure & Return

The tour concludes with a visit to the mangrove swamps, where guests discover the vital role these ecosystems play in protecting the island’s coastline. After exploring the diverse landscapes and enjoying moments of tranquility, guests return with a deeper appreciation of Zanzibar’s natural wonders.

Know Before You Go

What to Wear

Wear light, breathable clothing, comfortable closed walking shoes, and a hat. Modest attire covering shoulders and knees is appreciated in cultural sites.

Sun & Hydration

Bring sunscreen, sunglasses, and your camera. Cold bottled water is included throughout your guided experience.

Flexible Timing

Because this tour is private, we can schedule morning or afternoon departures to match your flight, tide, or resort itinerary.
